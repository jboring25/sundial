# Sundial

A Cubbie Skill. Text this repo's link to Cubbie to install it.

The most expensive thing a driven student loses is the opportunity they never saw. Sundial reads your
professors' emails, your Canvas announcements and the web, scores every opportunity against your dream job
(fit, how directly it leads there, pay) minus what it costs you (hours, money, travel), checks it against your
calendar, and texts you the three worth your time.

Try the ranker without Cubbie: `python3 scripts/rank.py --sample --top 5`

Built for the Cubbie Hackathon (Track 1).
